TotK Guesser – GeoGuessr for Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom

Where in Hyrule
are you?

One screenshot. One pin. From the Sky Islands down to the Depths – find the spot and score up to 5,000 points per image.

Difficulty

Surface, Sky Islands and Depths.

Same seed, same images – share it to challenge a friend.

How to play

  1. Look closely.Zoom into the screenshot and search for landmarks.
  2. Pick the layer.Sky, Surface or Depths – a wrong layer scores zero.
  3. Drop your pin.The closer you are, the more points you get.
